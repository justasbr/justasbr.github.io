How to read this page
ELO
A team strength rating (average = 1500). Higher is better. Updated after each game based on result and margin of victory. Ratings are compressed toward the mean to match market odds.
Home
Home Court Advantage — extra ELO points a team gets when playing at home. Estimated from 5 seasons of home vs away performance, with shrinkage toward the league average. Teams playing at displaced venues (e.g. in a different country) receive no home advantage for those games.
Top 6
Chance of finishing in the top 6 and going straight to the quarterfinals.
Play-in
Chance of finishing 7th–10th and needing to survive the play-in tournament to advance.
Out
Chance of finishing 11th–20th and being eliminated before the quarterfinals.
Quarterfinals
Chance of reaching the quarterfinals by any path (top 6 direct or winning through the play-in). The play-in is single-elimination: 7v8 (winner to QF), 9v10 (loser out), then the two remaining teams play for the last QF spot. Higher seed hosts each game.
Final 4
Chance of reaching the Final Four (semifinals). QF is BO5 (H,H,A,A,H), with matchups 1v8, 2v7, 3v6, 4v5.
Win title
Chance of winning the EuroLeague championship. Semifinals and Final are BO1 on neutral court.
This round
How a team's quarterfinal odds changed after the latest round. Green = odds improved, red = odds dropped. Small changes (<1%) are shown in grey.
Position grid
Each cell shows the probability (%) of a team finishing in that exact position. Darker purple = higher probability. Green and yellow lines mark 6th place and 10th place.
Schedules
Remaining games for each team with ELO-derived win probabilities and dates. Games highlighted with Neutral Venue are played at a displaced location (e.g. Israeli–Turkish matchups relocated to Belgrade or Sofia).
ELO model: K=20, team-specific home advantage, MOV-adjusted, 50% preseason prior, market-calibrated
