How to read this page
ELO
A team strength rating (average = 1500). Higher is better. Updated after each game based on result and margin of victory.
HCA
Home Court Advantage — extra ELO points a team gets when playing at home. Estimated from this season's home vs away performance. A higher number means the team is significantly harder to beat at home (e.g. Real Madrid +158).
Top 6
Chance of finishing in the top 6 and skipping the play-in — direct to the quarterfinals.
Play-in
Chance of finishing 7th–10th and needing to survive the play-in tournament to advance.
Out
Chance of finishing 11th–20th and being eliminated from the postseason.
Playoffs
Chance of reaching the quarterfinals by any path (top 6 direct or winning through the play-in). The play-in is single-elimination: 7v8 (winner to QF), 9v10 (loser out), then the two remaining teams play for the last QF spot. Higher seed hosts each game.
Position grid
Each cell shows the probability (%) of a team finishing in that exact position. Brighter purple = higher probability. Green and yellow lines mark the playoff (6th) and play-in (10th) cutoffs.
