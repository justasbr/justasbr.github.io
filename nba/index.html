How it works

  • Projections. Two independent sets of 2026-27 projections, weighted equally.
  • Player value. Per-game z-scores for the 9 categories over the top 182 players (14 teams × 13). FG% and FT% are weighted by attempts. TO counts against. Missed games count at replacement level.
  • H2H player value. The player's worst category (lowest per-game z) is dropped, because an H2H team can punt it. So a punt-FT% player like Giannis keeps his value. This value drives player ranks and the auction review.
  • Team strength. Sum of player value above replacement, per category.
  • Simulation. Each week every category gets random noise, plus one shared shock for games played that moves all counting categories together. A week is won by winning more categories. The real schedule after week 1 is not public yet, so later weeks use a random round robin.
  • Data. Rosters, auction and week 1 from Yahoo on 2026-10-11.
